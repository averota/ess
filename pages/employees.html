    Upload employees

    Preview a file, then append or overwrite the employees table.

    File needs columns Name, Gender (female/male), Position, Department, Business unit, and Hired date. Optional columns: Employee ID, Supervisor and Second line (their Employee ID or exact name), Probation end date, Last day, Role (user/admin, defaults to user), Email. Position/Department/Business unit values that don't already exist are created automatically. If a row sets a Supervisor or Second line, also fill in its own Employee ID — otherwise other rows can't link to it as their supervisor / second line. Rows with an Employee ID or Email that already exists are skipped by "Append" and are shown in the preview like any other row.
